During our synthetic AGV pickup trials, localization uncertainty correlated with retries. This association proves that localization uncertainty caused every failed pickup.

We plan repeated trials to compare two pickup settings. Randomized run order and equal replications at each level should help us investigate the response without confusing a trial sequence with a treatment effect.

Field observations about pickup failures should remain distinct from approved operating procedures. Peer review can assess the evidence, while supervisor approval determines whether a procedure is authorized for production.
